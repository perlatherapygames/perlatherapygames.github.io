How to play

  • Tap a tile to read its story first — then the question appears.
  • Every story has the same 5 questions: $100 Who · $200 What · $300 Where · $400 Why · $500 When.
  • You get 20 seconds per question. A correct pick adds the dollars to your score.
  • One secret tile is a Daily Double — bet your own dollars on it!
  • Finish the whole board to unlock Final Jeopardy: wager on one last main-idea question.
📱 Add this game to your Home Screen

On iPhone (Safari):

  1. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add — the Main Idea Jeopardy cover becomes the icon, and the game opens full-screen like an app.

On Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen / Install app.
